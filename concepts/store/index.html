The shelf

    Shipping

    Roast and ship
    Orders placed by Wednesday at noon are roasted Thursday and shipped Friday. Later orders go out the following week.
    Free over $40
    Orders of $40 or more ship free across the United States. Smaller orders ship for a flat $6.
    Arrives in 2 to 4 days
    Most orders arrive Saturday or Monday. You get a tracking email when your box leaves.

    Returns

    Not right? Tell us.
    If a coffee is not what you hoped for, write to us within 30 days and we will send a different bag or refund you.
    Gear returns
    Unused brewing gear can come back within 30 days in its box. We cover return shipping if an item arrives damaged.
