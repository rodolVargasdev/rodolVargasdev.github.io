A cook's corner, not a fancy room.

In the Basque Country a txoko is the back room where friends cook for each other. We built ours around one iron grill and a few rules.

Chef Iker Aldama grew up in a fishing town near San Sebastian and spent eleven years cooking in Boston before opening Txoko in 2021. The grill burns oak and apple wood only, split by a farm in Gorham, and it is lit at 3 p.m. every day.

We buy fish off the Portland docks the morning we cook it, bake our bread twice a day, and keep the wine list short and Spanish. If something is not good that week, it is not on the menu.
